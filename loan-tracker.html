Import from LINE

Paste your chat or upload the exported .txt. Importing replaces the current data so the dashboard always matches your latest LINE chat.

How to export your LINE chat
  1. Open the LINE chat → tap the ≡ menu (top-right).
  2. Choose Other settings → Export chat history / Export text.
  3. Save/share the .txt file, then upload or paste it here.
Each line containing a + or − number becomes an entry (+ = borrow, − = repay). Other lines are ignored.
